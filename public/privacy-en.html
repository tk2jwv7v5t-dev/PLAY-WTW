Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. What we store about you

  • Your account: username, display name, avatar color, and sign-up date.
  • Your password: stored encrypted, and nobody can see it, not even support.
  • Your messages and the photos you send: stored so they show up for you and the people with you. Photos are visible only to members of the same group or conversation.
  • The groups you're in, and the people you've blocked.
  • Reports: the reported message, who reported it, and the reason.
  • Notifications: if you turn them on, we store your device's address so we can send it notifications. Notifications are encrypted so only your device can read them.

2. What we don't store

  • Voice calls and screen sharing from a computer go directly between devices; we don't record or store them.
  • Phone screen sharing passes through our server to reach the people in your room, but it is not stored.
  • We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

3. Who can see your data

  • Your group members: your name, your messages in the group, and whether you're online.
  • Support: the user list (name, username, sign-up date, number of groups, and whether you're online). Support sees reported messages only, not your full conversations.
  • Service providers: the site is hosted on Cloudflare, which may log technical information such as your IP address for security.

4. On your device

We store your settings in your browser (language, theme, text size, and audio), plus a "cookie" that keeps you signed in for up to 30 days.

5. Deleting your data

When you delete your account, your account, your messages and the groups you created are deleted, and you leave all other groups.

6. Security

Passwords are encrypted, all connections are encrypted (HTTPS), and there is protection against password guessing and spam.

7. Changes

If we change this policy, we will announce it on the site.
